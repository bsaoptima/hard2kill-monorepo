## THE PLAYBOOK

### Step 1: Generate Hooks with Claude

**What it is:** Use Claude to batch-generate scroll-stopping hooks for your niche.

**Prompt template:**
```
I run a Discord server about [NICHE]. Generate 20 hooks for TikTok/Reels that:
- Lead with a bold claim or money/time number
- Create curiosity or controversy
- Are 2 lines max (hook + punch)
- Never sound like an ad

Examples of good hooks:
- "I mass $500/month from my Discord server"
- "Everyone builds communities wrong. Here's why."
```

**Output:** 20 hooks → pick top 5 → rotate daily

**Time:** 10 minutes/week

### Step 3: Generate AI UGC Content on Pletor

**What Pletor does:** Creates AI-generated talking-head videos with your script. Looks like real UGC without filming yourself.

**Workflow:**
1. Take your Claude-generated hook
2. Write 30-60 second script (hook → problem → solution → CTA)
3. Paste into Pletor
4. Select avatar (match your target demo)
5. Generate → download → post

**Script formula:**
```
[HOOK - 3 seconds]
"Setup to bring 100 users per day to your Discord server"

[PROBLEM - 10 seconds]
"Most people post once a week and wonder why no one joins.
The algorithm buries you. Your server stays dead."

[SOLUTION - 30 seconds]
"Here's what actually works:
1. Use Claude to generate 20 hooks per week
2. Batch-create AI videos on Pletor
3. Post 5 times a day across TikTok, Reels, Shorts
4. Reply to every comment - that's where conversions happen
5. Send them to a simple landing or straight to Discord"

[CTA - 5 seconds]
"Comment 'DISCORD' and I'll send you the full workflow"
```

**Output:** 5 videos/week minimum

---

### Step 4: Post 5 Times a Day

**Platform split:**
| Platform | Posts/day | Best times (EST) |
|----------|-----------|------------------|
| TikTok | 2 | 7am, 7pm |
| Instagram Reels | 2 | 12pm, 6pm |
| YouTube Shorts | 1 | 3pm |

**Cross-posting rules:**
- Same video, different caption
- Stagger by 2+ hours
- Different cover frame for each platform
- Add +0.5s intro padding for Reels (avoids duplicate detection)

**Engagement protocol:**
- Reply to EVERY comment in first 60 minutes
- Pin controversial or high-engagement comments
- Like all comments (signals activity to algorithm)
- DM anyone who comments your trigger word

---

### Step 5: Convert to Server Users

**Funnel:**
```
TikTok/Reels → Comment trigger → DM → Discord link → Server
```

**Comment trigger strategy:**

In video CTA: "Comment 'DISCORD' and I'll send you the invite"

Auto-DM template:
```
Hey! Here's the Discord: [LINK]

When you join:
1. Intro yourself in #introductions
2. Check #start-here for the guide
3. Drop any questions in #general

See you inside 🔥
```

**Why this works:**
- Comment = engagement boost = more reach
- DM = personal touch = higher conversion
- Direct link in DM avoids "link in bio" friction

**Server onboarding:**
- #welcome channel with clear value prop
- #start-here with 3-step quickstart
- Auto-role on join
- First 24-hour ping with exclusive content

## WHAT NOT TO DO

- ❌ Post once a day and expect growth
- ❌ Put Discord link in bio and hope people click
- ❌ Ignore comments (kills your reach)
- ❌ Same exact video on all platforms at same time
- ❌ Generic hooks ("Join my Discord!")
- ❌ Long videos (keep under 60 seconds)
- ❌ Skip the DM step (biggest conversion lever)
